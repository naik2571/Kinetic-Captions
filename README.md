# 🎬 Kinetic Captions: Synchronized Typography Studio

A creator-grade, 100% offline, on-device kinetic caption and dynamic subtitle engine for Android. Renders viral, high-retention word-by-word animations overlaid directly onto video at 60 FPS without uploading footage to cloud servers.

---

## ⚡ Kinetic Animation Styles

1. **🔥 Pop & Bounce:** Dynamic spring physics overshoot scale (`1.0x -> 1.35x`) on active words with electric yellow typography.
2. **🎤 Karaoke Pill:** High-contrast cyan/yellow rounded badge highlighting each spoken word with dimmed background text.
3. **⚡ Typewriter:** Millisecond-accurate character-by-character punch-in with blinking cursor sync.
4. **✨ Neon Pulse:** Vibrating multi-pass neon stroke outline with radiant outer glow.

---

## 🛠️ Key Capabilities

- **Microsecond Audio/Video Sync:** Sub-frame accurate timeline scrubbing with live millisecond timecode (`00:03.4s / 00:08.5s`).
- **Built-in Procedural Canvas Backdrop:** Instant real-time preview even without pre-existing video clips.
- **Preloaded Viral Hook & Tech Presets:** Ready-to-use retention-optimized short-form video pacing (2 to 4 words per burst).
- **100% On-Device & Zero Cloud:** Complete creator privacy—no video frames or audio ever leave the device.

---

## 📦 Tech Stack

- **Platform:** Android Native (Kotlin 1.9.23, Android SDK 34)
- **Graphics Engine:** Hardware-accelerated custom Canvas, PorterDuff shaders, math-based spring curves
- **UI:** Material 3, ViewBinding, synchronized RecyclerView timeline
